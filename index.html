How fidélisation works

1. Customer scans you. 2. You scan customer. Streak grows.

The customer scans your business QR once to get his fidelity QR in Google Wallet. Every visit after that, you scan his code and his stamps, points and streak build up — with bonus points on Christmas & promos pushed straight to his wallet.

Customer returns → scan his code → streak starts

1 stamp + 10 pts per visit. Christmas/New Year = +1 extra stamp & +20 pts. Weekends +10. Streak 5+ = +20. 10 stamps = 🎁 reward. Promo notifications land in his wallet.
